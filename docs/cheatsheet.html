Keys & switch

Keysplay (two octaves)
⇧ + white keyload patch 1–15 · hold 1 s to save (blinks red)
PLAYarpeggiator on / off
LOOPlatch the arp · sustain when the arp is off
⇧ + PLAY / LOOPoctave down / up
Toggle switchup = mono (glide, unison) · down = poly (6 voices)
⇧ + PLAY + LOOP at power‑onbattery off (shipping mode)

White keys show the value you're turning

bar from the left: normal knob
bar from the middle: ± knob (middle = zero)
one key: a stepped choice (wave, octave, shape…)

Your sound is saved by itself and comes back at power‑on.
Lost? ⇧ + click resets a knob. Click volume = all notes off.
